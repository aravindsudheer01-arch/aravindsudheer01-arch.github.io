ABOUT ARAVIND

One engineering mindset.
More than one medium.

I’m Aravind Sudheer, a Robotics & Mechatronics Engineering graduate from Monash University with an AI specialisation. I am also registered as a Graduate Engineer (Mechatronics) with the Board of Engineers Malaysia.

My professional background is in industrial robotics and automation: programming ABB robots, calibrating physical systems, checking signals and troubleshooting execution.

At Simpliforge I helped coordinate site operations and collaborated with the CTO on training and safety documents. At Automation Systems and Services, I worked with fellow interns to deliver a Pick-to-Light system ahead of deadline and introduced a better equipment storage process.

My newer work uses Python, SQL and applied AI to build applications and analyse data. The tools have changed, but the engineering question stays useful: what does the system do, how do its parts connect, and what evidence shows that it works?

I’m looking for junior Python development, AI application and data analysis roles, alongside selected graduate ML and computer vision opportunities. I want to use my engineering experience—not discard it—as I move further into software.
