## The problem
A robot needed to locate and place coloured pucks into geometric patterns during a timed arena task. The mechanical system had to support object handling within a compact physical arrangement.

## My contribution
As the mechanical design lead, I designed a two-degree-of-freedom arm and gripper in SOLIDWORKS. I managed 3D printing of the mechanical components and worked on assembly and mechanism validation.

This was an academic team project. My highlighted contribution is mechanical design and fabrication, not sole ownership of every sensing, navigation or control component.

## Technical approach
The work connected CAD modelling with manufacturable parts, assembly and physical object handling. The arm and gripper needed to balance reach, compactness and the requirements of the task.

## Results & evidence
The project progressed from a CAD design to printed and assembled components, followed by object-handling validation. It provided practical experience with the gap between a digital assembly and a functioning physical mechanism.
