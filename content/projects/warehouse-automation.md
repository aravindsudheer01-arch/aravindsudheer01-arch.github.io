## The engineering context
Warehouse automation depends on agreement between electrical connections, sensors, controller signals and operator interfaces. Small integration errors can prevent an otherwise complete system from being ready for use.

## My contribution
During my internship at Automation Systems and Services in Kuala Lumpur, I created electrical schematics in AutoCAD, worked on control-panel wiring and developed WinCC SCADA warehouse layouts.

I supported sensor installation, PLC signal verification, electrical checks, system validation and technical documentation. I also helped reorganise packing and setup workflows during schedule-sensitive deployments.

## Technical approach
The work combined wiring and sensor checks with signal-level validation and interface preparation. It included support for warehouse automation and Pick-to-Light deployment rather than ownership of an entire production control system.

## Results & evidence
The internship provided experience connecting design work with physical installation and functional checks. It also developed my ability to communicate with technicians and engineers during practical deployment work.

## Scope & limitations
I supported industrial-system commissioning. I did **not** independently commission a complete SCADA system. This distinction is important when describing the scope of my experience.
