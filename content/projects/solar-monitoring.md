## The problem
A solar installation needs measurements that make its operating state understandable. This final-year project connected hardware measurements with a remote monitoring interface, rather than treating sensing and software as separate tasks.

## My contribution
Under industry guidance from Aiszzy Electronics, I worked on hardware integration, prototype assembly and the monitoring workflow. The work included system planning, integration flowcharts, risk assessment and rooftop validation.

This was an **industry-guided academic project**. My Aiszzy involvement was associated with this project; it is not listed here as a separate employment period.

## Technical approach
Arduino-based hardware and Blynk connected measured voltage, current, power and temperature to a monitoring interface. Integration involved the physical prototype as well as the path from measurement to displayed information.

The project was planned using Gantt charts and risk assessments. Rooftop testing provided a practical setting for checking the assembled hardware–software system under industry guidance.

## Results & evidence
The project reached an assembled prototype and rooftop validation. It gave me experience connecting electronics, data collection and user-facing monitoring, and identifying integration issues across those boundaries.

The schematic is intentionally high-level. It does not reproduce a client wiring diagram or imply that the shown arrangement is a complete electrical design.
