## The problem
An automated resistor-sorting system needs repeatable sequencing: observe an input, make the sorting decision and actuate the appropriate mechanism.

## My contribution
As team coordinator, I developed PLC ladder logic for automated sequencing and sensor-based sorting. I performed I/O validation, sensor calibration and functional testing of the system.

## Technical approach
The work focused on the interaction between input signals, controller states and actuator outputs. Calibration and signal verification helped check whether the physical process matched the intended sequence.

## Results & evidence
The completed academic work combined PLC programming with hands-on validation. It is an example of practical control logic and system integration, not a trained ML classifier.

## Limitations
No sorting-accuracy, throughput or labour-saving figure is claimed. The generic diagram is not an electrical schematic for construction. PLC hardware details and photographs are not included in this first public version.
