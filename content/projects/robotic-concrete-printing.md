## The engineering context
Robotic concrete printing connects programmed motion to a physical manufacturing process. A simulated path still needs the correct workobject, tooling, operating parameters and checks before execution.

## My contribution
As a Junior Robotics Engineer at Simpliforge Creations, I programmed, simulated and deployed ABB six-axis robots using RAPID and RobotStudio. I configured workobjects, tool data and end-effectors before live print operations.

I monitored robot motion, PLC signals and process parameters, and troubleshot motion and program-execution issues. I also maintained project reporting and supported safe site operations.

## Technical approach
The work moved between simulation, configuration and live execution. Coordinate references and tool configuration needed to match the physical setup. Observing actual behavior and checking signals helped narrow down execution issues.

This was professional industrial robotics work. It was not professional ML model development or a Python software-engineering role.

## Results & evidence
The role provided hands-on experience with ABB robot programming, calibration and execution in an additive-manufacturing environment. The evidence here describes the responsibilities recorded in my CV, not a quantified claim about production improvement.
