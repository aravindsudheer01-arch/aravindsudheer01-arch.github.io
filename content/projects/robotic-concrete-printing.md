## The engineering context

Robotic concrete 3D printing requires more than a simulated toolpath. The robot's workobject, tooling, motion program and physical setup must agree before a concrete extrusion can run safely and reliably on site. This work took place during my professional role at **Simpliforge Creations** in Hyderabad.

## My role and responsibilities

I programmed, simulated and operated **ABB six-axis industrial robots** using **RAPID** and **RobotStudio** for concrete additive manufacturing. Before execution, I configured and calibrated workobjects, tool data and end-effectors, then supported live robotic printing.

I monitored robotic motion, PLC signals and operating parameters, troubleshot motion and program-execution issues, and maintained site and production reports.

## Leadership and engineering documentation

I was appointed **second-in-charge of site operations** within my first month, supporting daily coordination and working with other site personnel. I also created training material for incoming junior engineers and collaborated with the company's CTO on **safety guidance and onboarding documentation**.

These responsibilities strengthened my ability to translate hands-on engineering knowledge into procedures that others can follow.

## What this experience taught me

This role connected simulation and robot programming to physical manufacturing and real site constraints. A toolpath can appear correct in software yet still require calibration, troubleshooting and careful coordination before it can produce a usable object.

It also gave me practical responsibility beyond programming: supervising site work, helping engineers learn procedures, and contributing to safe, repeatable operations.

## Publication boundaries and limitations

The photographs and video are personal work samples, not a comprehensive account of company production. I have not published proprietary **RAPID programs**, detailed machine configurations, client drawings, confidential manufacturing parameters or throughput claims.
